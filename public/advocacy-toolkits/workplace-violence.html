Workplace violence — hospital safety toolkit

Brief member hospitals on staff safety advocacy. All content requires association policy and legal review before external use.

Why members care

  • Unified reporting and training expectations across member facilities.
  • Legislative alerts when safety bills advance in state or federal sessions.
  • Training completion tracking tied to Learn modules (alpha).

Staff workflow in PulsePoint (alpha)

  1. Publish issue page with hero media and this toolkit.
  2. Link mandatory training playlists in Learn.
  3. Launch take-action when bill movement triggers outreach.
  4. Segment Engage by hospital executives and nurse managers.

Before you share externally

  • Verify statutory references with policy counsel — do not invent legal claims.
  • Use only association-approved incident or workforce statistics.
  • PAC/FEC boundaries: grassroots hospital sign-on only — not PAC fundraising.
